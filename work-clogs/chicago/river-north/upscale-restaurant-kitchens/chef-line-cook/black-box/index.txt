<h2 id="where-river-north-eats">Where River North eats</h2>
<p>Galleries fill the old industrial buildings, and the evenings run late. Choose Chicago, which handles tourism for the city, calls River North an &quot;ultra-stylish, ultra-urban district&quot; and adds that it &quot;shifts into high gear by night&quot; (<a href="https://www.choosechicago.com/neighborhoods/river-north/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Choose Chicago</a>). Its guide points to &quot;one of the city&#39;s hottest nightlife scenes&quot; in the same neighborhood.</p>
<p>Dinner is a big part of the draw. Among the spots Choose Chicago highlights are steakhouses, riverside restaurants and kitchens run by celebrity chefs, and it says the dining scene &quot;is always buzzing.&quot; City Colleges of Chicago&#39;s community research guide counts River North as one piece of the Near North Side community area (<a href="https://researchguides.ccc.edu/community_survey/Central" rel="noopener" target="_blank">CCC</a>).</p>
<p>Hours differ from one restaurant to the next, and we have no inside view of any particular one. Even so, a dining room that stays full into the night keeps its cooks standing for a long time. Choose footwear for the heaviest night on the calendar, not the lightest.</p>
<h2 id="behind-the-pass-the-chef">Behind the pass: the chef</h2>
<p>A chef rarely stands still for long. Expediting, checking plates, stepping into the cooler for a count and walking the line to see how each cook is keeping up, she may cross the kitchen dozens of times in one service. She&#39;s often the person who hands a new hire the dress code, shoes included.</p>
<p>So she tends to judge a clog on two questions: will it still feel good in the twelfth hour, and does it pass the restaurant&#39;s own footwear policy?</p>
<h2 id="at-the-station-the-line-cook">At the station: the line cook</h2>
<p>A line cook&#39;s territory is a few tiles wide. Range, board and reach-in are an arm&#39;s length apart, and during a push she rotates between them nonstop while the cooks on either side do the same.</p>
<p>Her checklist for a clog is short: enough space up front so her toes aren&#39;t cramped and leather care that&#39;s realistic after a late close.</p>
<h2 id="buying-a-womens-kitchen-clog-what-to-weigh">Buying a women&#39;s kitchen clog: what to weigh</h2>
<p>The restaurant&#39;s footwear policy comes first. With it in hand, compare listings on these points.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Written maker claims about the sole.</strong> If the policy calls for a specific sole rating, choose a maker that states that rating, and compare it with the policy. No rating makes a fall impossible.</li>
<li><strong>A back that holds the heel, a front that covers the foot.</strong> Closed-back clogs keep the heel in place as you turn. For safety-toe requirements, consider only listings that state a safety-toe rating.</li>
<li><strong>Leather care that fits your routine.</strong> Different leathers call for different care, and the maker&#39;s care note tells you which.</li>
<li><strong>Arch support you can stand on.</strong> For shifts on your feet, look for a contoured footbed with arch support built in.</li>
<li><strong>Women&#39;s sizing in the right width.</strong> Size from the women&#39;s chart, and confirm the maker offers your width in the color you want.</li>
</ul>
<p><em>We don&#39;t give medical advice. A podiatrist is the right person to see if your feet hurt after shifts or you have a condition like plantar fasciitis.</em></p>

<p>Dansko&#39;s Black Box page explains that its shoes follow &quot;European (EU) whole size standards,&quot; so pick your size from Dansko&#39;s women&#39;s chart. <p>The miles she covers in a night put the rocker bottom and the laterally stable heel counter to use, as Dansko describes them. Smooth black leather also suits someone who moves between the kitchen and the dining room. Check the restaurant&#39;s written policy before she wears it on the line.</p>
