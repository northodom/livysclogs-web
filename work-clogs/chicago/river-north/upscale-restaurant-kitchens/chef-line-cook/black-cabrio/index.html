What she wants from a clog is simple to say and hard to find: a front that doesn't pinch the toes and a care routine she can manage after a closing shift.

A women's kitchen clog checklist

Read the restaurant's footwear policy before anything else. Then sort the options with these points.

  • The maker's own words about the outsole. If the policy names a particular sole standard, look for a maker that makes that claim in writing, and hold the listing up against the policy. A written claim still isn't a guarantee against falls.
  • A closed heel and a covered foot. A closed back holds the heel through quick turns. If your workplace asks for a safety toe, stick to listings that state a safety-toe rating.
  • A leather care routine you'll keep. Leather needs looking after. Check what the maker says about care before you order.
  • Built-in arch support. A contoured footbed is worth prioritizing for long hours standing.
  • Women's sizing, and the width you need. Use the women's size chart, and see which widths the maker offers in that exact color.

This page is not medical advice. If shifts leave your feet sore, or you have a condition such as plantar fasciitis, talk to a podiatrist.

Footbed and arch

Heel height and widths

The Black Cabrio measurements are a 2 in heel height and a 0.8 in platform height. That lift takes a little getting used to, so give the clogs a few hours at home first. Dansko's width selector for Black Cabrio offers Regular and Wide; check that your width is actually available on the Amazon listing.
