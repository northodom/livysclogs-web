Method

How we rank clogs

Every ranking on this site comes from Amazon's public Best Sellers lists. What you see is an Amazon Best Sellers snapshot from September 25, 2026; Amazon's own lists change often, so check Amazon for the current order. We never make up or hand-adjust an order. We haven't tried these clogs ourselves.

1. Which lists we use

Livy's is a specialist site for restaurant and food-service crews, so Amazon's Health Care & Food Service lists (women's and men's work shoes) are the lists we show. Our Chef & Kitchen page also includes listings from Amazon's general women's and men's clog lists whose titles mention chef, kitchen, or food-service use. In each list we keep listings Amazon titles as clogs.

2. Brands in a list

Brands are ordered by the best position any of their listings holds in that list's top 100, then by how many listings they have there. Listings whose brand we couldn't confirm are shown separately, not ranked.

3. Styles in a brand

A brand's listings are shown in Amazon Best Sellers order.

Premium brands are picks, not rankings

The Premium Brands row is hand-picked by us and listed A–Z. It isn't based on sales. Brands that aren't in a snapshot's top-100 lists are labeled unranked.

Data checks

The site won't build if the snapshot is empty or malformed. Collection is paused, so the lists stay a dated snapshot until we switch to a sanctioned data source. Family-friendly note: holiday-themed listings are left off.
