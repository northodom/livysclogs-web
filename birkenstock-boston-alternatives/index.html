What to compare in any Boston look-alike

  • Upper: real suede vs. faux suede (check the listing's "fabric type").
  • Footbed: cork footbeds mold to your foot over time; foam feels soft right away.
  • Sole: EVA is light; rubber grips better on wet ground.
  • Strap: an adjustable buckle helps if you want to wear thick socks.
  • Reviews: read the newest reviews for sizing notes before you pick a size.
