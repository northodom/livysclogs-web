Dansko is the name a lot of nurses, chefs, and teachers say first when you ask about work clogs. But the lineup goes past the famous Professional. There are lighter slip-resistant clogs, a platform, open-back mules, and easy foam styles. Here's how they compare.

How we wrote this: we haven't wear-tested every style. This guide is based on Dansko's own product, clog, and sizing guides and the current Amazon listings, with sources linked.

Quick picks

  • The classic: Dansko Professional
  • Best lighter, slip-resistant pick: Dansko XP 2.0
  • Best for height: Dansko Platform Pro
  • Best easy-clean casual clog: Dansko Kane

First, how Dansko sizing works

Dansko sizes in European whole sizes. On its size chart, a women's US 7.5 to 8 is a 38, and a women's US 8.5 to 9 is a 39 (Dansko size chart). For closed-back clogs like the Professional and XP 2.0, Dansko says:

  • the fit may feel slightly snug when you step in
  • your toes shouldn't touch the front
  • your heel should move up and down freely as you walk

Dansko also suggests leaving about a pinky's width between your heel and the back of the clog. Most styles come in medium width, and some come in narrow and wide. Our Dansko Professional review goes deeper on fit.

The work clogs

Dansko Professional

Dansko calls it its "flagship clog." Inside are a roomy reinforced toe box, a contoured PU midsole, an inner frame for stability, a padded instep collar, and an anti-fatigue rocker bottom. The listing notes leather, fabric, or TPU uppers. Dansko lists a 2-inch heel on its product pages.

In its clog guide, Dansko describes the Professional as the original, with "a firmer footbed and a more structured feel" (Dansko clog guide).

Best for: people who like firm, structured support and a classic look.

Dansko XP 2.0

Dansko's clog guide says the XP 2.0 has "a softer feel underfoot" and is lighter than the Professional. Per the listing, it has:

  • an EVA midsole
  • a removable dual-density footbed with memory foam that fits most orthotics
  • a 1.75-inch heel
  • a slip-resistant rubber outsole for dry, wet, and oily/wet surfaces

It also comes in a men's version.

Best for: long shifts on slick floors, and anyone who wants cushioning from the first step.

Dansko LT Pro

The LT Pro is another lighter-weight classic clog. Its listing describes an EVA midsole and a removable memory foam footbed, much like the XP 2.0. Its listing doesn't mention a slip-resistant outsole, so if your job requires one, choose the XP 2.0 or check the LT Pro's details first.

Dansko Platform Pro

This is a newer take on the heritage shape with a chunky platform. The listing describes a contoured memory foam footbed and a slip-resistant rubber sole for dry, wet, and oily/wet floors.

Best for: a bit of height at work. For more platforms, see our platform clog guide.

The open-back and slingback styles

  • Ingrid: the listing calls it "a sling-back version of our flagship open-back clog." The strap can go behind your heel or over your foot.
  • Sonja: an open-back mule with a padded instep collar, a roomy toe box, and a rocker-bottom sole, per its listing.

For open-back styles, Dansko says your heel may meet the back edge exactly or sit slightly forward. Both are normal.

The casual foam clogs

Dansko Kane

The Kane is a slip-on mule with injected EVA uppers. Per the listing, it has perforations for ventilation, wipe-clean uppers, and a removable EVA footbed with Dansko Natural Arch support.

Best for: gardening, errands, and around-the-house wear.

Check the Kane on AmazonOpens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there

For work: the Kaci is listed as an occupational mule. It's made with bio-based EVA and has a slip-resistant rubber outsole, and the listing names healthcare, food service, and landscaping.

The dressier mules

The Berry and Baylor are leather slip-on mules with Dansko's stapled construction. Their listings describe a swivel instep strap with a buckle, a memory foam footbed, and a lightweight EVA outsole. They're good picks when you want Dansko comfort in a shoe that works with jeans or a dress.

Which Dansko should you get?

  • Classic, firm support: Professional.
  • Softer and slip-resistant: XP 2.0.
  • A little height at work: Platform Pro.
  • Easy, wipe-clean weekends: Kane.
  • Everyday outfits: Berry or Baylor.
