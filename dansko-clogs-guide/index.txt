13:T2014,<p>Dansko is the name a lot of nurses, chefs, and teachers say first when you ask about work clogs. But the lineup goes past the famous Professional. There are lighter slip-resistant clogs, a platform, open-back mules, and easy foam styles. Here&#39;s how they compare.</p>
<p><em>How we wrote this: we haven&#39;t wear-tested every style. This guide is based on Dansko&#39;s own product, clog, and sizing guides and the current Amazon listings, with sources linked.</em></p>
<p><em>Livy&#39;s Clogs isn&#39;t a medical provider. If you have foot pain, talk with a podiatrist.</em></p>
<h2 id="quick-picks">Quick picks</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>The classic:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003VVTU38?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Dansko Professional</a></li>
<li><strong>Best lighter, slip-resistant pick:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B083TTWRCK?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Dansko XP 2.0</a></li>
<li><strong>Best for height:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQQXKBCB?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Dansko Platform Pro</a></li>
<li><strong>Best easy-clean casual clog:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BQCZ5WGB?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Dansko Kane</a></li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="first-how-dansko-sizing-works">First, how Dansko sizing works</h2>
<p>Dansko sizes in <strong>European whole sizes</strong>. On its size chart, a women&#39;s US 7.5 to 8 is a 38, and a women&#39;s US 8.5 to 9 is a 39 (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/size-charts" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko size chart</a>). For closed-back clogs like the Professional and XP 2.0, Dansko says:</p>
<ul>
<li>the fit may feel slightly snug when you step in</li>
<li>your toes shouldn&#39;t touch the front</li>
<li>your heel should move up and down freely as you walk</li>
</ul>
<p>Dansko also suggests leaving about a pinky&#39;s width between your heel and the back of the clog. Most styles come in medium width, and some come in narrow and wide. Our <a href="/dansko-professional-review/">Dansko Professional review</a> goes deeper on fit.</p>
<h2 id="the-work-clogs">The work clogs</h2>
<h3 id="dansko-professional">Dansko Professional</h3>
<p>Dansko calls it its &quot;flagship clog.&quot; Inside are a roomy reinforced toe box, a contoured PU midsole, an inner frame for stability, a padded instep collar, and an anti-fatigue rocker bottom. The listing notes leather, fabric, or TPU uppers. Dansko lists a 2-inch heel on its product pages.</p>
<p>In its clog guide, Dansko describes the Professional as the original, with &quot;a firmer footbed and a more structured feel&quot; (<a href="https://dansko.com/pages/clog-guide" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Dansko clog guide</a>).</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> people who like firm, structured support and a classic look.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003VVTU38?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Professional on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h3 id="dansko-xp-2-0">Dansko XP 2.0</h3>
<p>Dansko&#39;s clog guide says the XP 2.0 has &quot;a softer feel underfoot&quot; and is lighter than the Professional. Per the listing, it has:</p>
<ul>
<li>an EVA midsole</li>
<li>a removable dual-density footbed with memory foam that fits most orthotics</li>
<li>a 1.75-inch heel</li>
<li>a slip-resistant rubber outsole for dry, wet, and oily/wet surfaces</li>
</ul>
<p>It also comes in a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D6L8Q6DF?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">men&#39;s version</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> long shifts on slick floors, and anyone who wants cushioning from the first step.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B083TTWRCK?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the XP 2.0 on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h3 id="dansko-lt-pro">Dansko LT Pro</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08BBDLVJ3?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">LT Pro</a> is another lighter-weight classic clog. Its listing describes an EVA midsole and a removable memory foam footbed, much like the XP 2.0. Its listing doesn&#39;t mention a slip-resistant outsole, so if your job requires one, choose the XP 2.0 or check the LT Pro&#39;s details first.</p>
<h3 id="dansko-platform-pro">Dansko Platform Pro</h3>
<p>This is a newer take on the heritage shape with a chunky platform. The listing describes a contoured memory foam footbed and a slip-resistant rubber sole for dry, wet, and oily/wet floors.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> a bit of height at work. For more platforms, see our <a href="/best-platform-clogs/">platform clog guide</a>.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQQXKBCB?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Platform Pro on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h2 id="the-open-back-and-slingback-styles">The open-back and slingback styles</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001EJN22Y?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Ingrid</a>:</strong> the listing calls it &quot;a sling-back version of our flagship open-back clog.&quot; The strap can go behind your heel or over your foot.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001EJN1NE?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Sonja</a>:</strong> an open-back mule with a padded instep collar, a roomy toe box, and a rocker-bottom sole, per its listing.</li>
</ul>
<p>For open-back styles, Dansko says your heel may meet the back edge exactly or sit slightly forward. Both are normal.</p>
<h2 id="the-casual-foam-clogs">The casual foam clogs</h2>
<h3 id="dansko-kane">Dansko Kane</h3>
<p>The Kane is a slip-on mule with injected EVA uppers. Per the listing, it has perforations for ventilation, wipe-clean uppers, and a removable EVA footbed with Dansko Natural Arch support.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> gardening, errands, and around-the-house wear.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BQCZ5WGB?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Kane on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<p><strong>For work:</strong> the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CQ2Y5KWY?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Kaci</a> is listed as an occupational mule. It&#39;s made with bio-based EVA and has a slip-resistant rubber outsole, and the listing names healthcare, food service, and landscaping.</p>
<h2 id="the-dressier-mules">The dressier mules</h2>
<p>The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07M7P8DN2?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Berry</a> and <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D6L7BZP3?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Baylor</a> are leather slip-on mules with Dansko&#39;s stapled construction. Their listings describe a swivel instep strap with a buckle, a memory foam footbed, and a lightweight EVA outsole. They&#39;re good picks when you want Dansko comfort in a shoe that works with jeans or a dress.</p>
<h2 id="which-dansko-should-you-get">Which Dansko should you get?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Classic, firm support:</strong> Professional.</li>
<li><strong>Softer and slip-resistant:</strong> XP 2.0.</li>
<li><strong>A little height at work:</strong> Platform Pro.</li>
<li><strong>Easy, wipe-clean weekends:</strong> Kane.</li>
<li><strong>Everyday outfits:</strong> Berry or Baylor.</li>
</ul>
