How Jibbitz work

Jibbitz are small charms on a post that pops into the holes of your Crocs. According to Crocs, each Classic Clog has 13 holes on top, so a pair can hold up to 26 charms (Crocs Jibbitz FAQ).

  • To put one on: squeeze the top of the clog into a loose fold, slide one side of the post into a hole, and press the rest in until it pops.
  • To take one off: fold the top around the hole again and ease the post out sideways. Don't yank straight up.
  • Other backers: Crocs also sells clip, tie, and pin versions for laces, sandal straps, and bags. Crocs says the backers aren't removable or interchangeable, so an original charm stays an original charm.

Many Crocs styles take charms. The Classic Clog is the classic canvas, and the Amazon listings for the Classic Lined and Classic Platform both mention Jibbitz. The platform listing says charms fit on the backstrap too.

How to style Jibbitz

  1. Pick a color story. Choose two or three charm colors that match your clog. White and pastel charms look fresh on black or navy. Bright charms pop on white or bone.
  2. Cluster, don't scatter. Grouping three to five charms near the toe looks deliberate. Filling all 13 holes is fun too. Just be sure you mean it!
  3. Try one statement charm. On neutral or lined clogs, a single flower or favorite character near the strap looks clean.
  4. Tell a little story. Mix a hobby, a favorite animal, and a food charm. It's a fun conversation starter.
  5. Match the family. Give everyone the same theme in different colors. Kids can trade to swap styles, an idea the Girls' Favorites listing mentions too.

Care tips

Pop charms out before a deep clean so dirt doesn't build up under them. Crocs warns that extreme heat, like a hot car, can shrink or warp its shoes (Crocs FAQ), so don't leave charmed pairs on a sunny dashboard.

Official vs. generic charms

Amazon also sells lots of generic "shoe charms" that aren't Jibbitz. Some are fine, but fit and quality vary, so read the listing and check the age warnings. The packs above are all sold by Crocs.
