<h2 id="how-jibbitz-work">How Jibbitz work</h2>
<p>Jibbitz are small charms on a post that pops into the holes of your Crocs. According to Crocs, each Classic Clog has 13 holes on top, so a pair can hold up to 26 charms (<a href="https://www.crocs.com.au/jibbitz-backings/jibbitz-backings.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Jibbitz FAQ</a>).</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>To put one on:</strong> squeeze the top of the clog into a loose fold, slide one side of the post into a hole, and press the rest in until it pops.</li>
<li><strong>To take one off:</strong> fold the top around the hole again and ease the post out sideways. Don&#39;t yank straight up.</li>
<li><strong>Other backers:</strong> Crocs also sells clip, tie, and pin versions for laces, sandal straps, and bags. Crocs says the backers aren&#39;t removable or interchangeable, so an original charm stays an original charm.</li>
</ul>
<p>Many Crocs styles take charms. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001526RFY?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Classic Clog</a> is the classic canvas, and the Amazon listings for the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09P1HTTSJ?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Classic Lined</a> and <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B088ZWHRKH?tag=livysclogs-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Classic Platform</a> both mention Jibbitz. The platform listing says charms fit on the backstrap too.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-style-jibbitz">How to style Jibbitz</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Pick a color story.</strong> Choose two or three charm colors that match your clog. White and pastel charms look fresh on black or navy. Bright charms pop on white or bone.</li>
<li><strong>Cluster, don&#39;t scatter.</strong> Grouping three to five charms near the toe looks deliberate. Filling all 13 holes is fun too. Just be sure you mean it!</li>
<li><strong>Try one statement charm.</strong> On neutral or <a href="/crocs-classic-lined-clog-review/">lined clogs</a>, a single flower or favorite character near the strap looks clean.</li>
<li><strong>Tell a little story.</strong> Mix a hobby, a favorite animal, and a food charm. It&#39;s a fun conversation starter.</li>
<li><strong>Match the family.</strong> Give everyone the same theme in different colors. Kids can trade to swap styles, an idea the Girls&#39; Favorites listing mentions too.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="care-tips">Care tips</h2>
<p>Pop charms out before a deep clean so dirt doesn&#39;t build up under them. Crocs warns that extreme heat, like a hot car, can shrink or warp its shoes (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/customer-service/faqs.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs FAQ</a>), so don&#39;t leave charmed pairs on a sunny dashboard.</p>
<h2 id="official-vs-generic-charms">Official vs. generic charms</h2>
<p>Amazon also sells lots of generic &quot;shoe charms&quot; that aren&#39;t Jibbitz. Some are fine, but fit and quality vary, so read the listing and check the age warnings. The packs above are all sold by Crocs.</p>
