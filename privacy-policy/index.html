Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you visit www.livysclogs.com (the "Site"), run by Livy's Clogs ("we," "us," or "our"). The short version: we don't collect personal information ourselves.

What we don't do

  • No analytics. We don't use Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking tool.
  • No email list and no forms. There is no newsletter, sign-up, comment, or contact form on the Site.
  • No cookies of our own. The Site doesn't set cookies, so there is no cookie banner.
  • No advertising networks. We don't show ads or use ad-tracking pixels.
  • We don't sell personal information.

Hosting: GitHub Pages

The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub's servers process technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request, and GitHub may keep this in server logs for security and operations. We don't receive or use these logs for tracking. GitHub's handling of this data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Site's fonts are served from the Site itself, not from a third-party font service.

Amazon affiliate links

We participate in the Amazon Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon.com include our Associates tag. When you click one and go to Amazon, Amazon may set cookies in your browser so it can credit a qualifying purchase to us. Those cookies are set and controlled by Amazon, not by us, and Amazon's use of information is covered by the Amazon Privacy Notice. We don't receive your name, address, or payment details from Amazon.

Links to other websites

The Site links to other websites, including Amazon and brand or source websites. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we add anything that changes what's described here (for example, analytics, an email list, or a contact form), we'll update this policy and its effective date before or when that change goes live.
