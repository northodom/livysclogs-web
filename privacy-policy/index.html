Information we collect

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them in a few ways:

  • Analytics cookies. We use [Google Analytics / analytics tool name] to understand how visitors use the Site, such as which articles are popular, so we can make it better. This service may set cookies and collect usage information as described above. You can learn how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Amazon affiliate cookies. We participate in the Amazon Associates Program. When you click a link to Amazon on our Site, Amazon may place a cookie on your browser so it can tell that you came from our Site and credit us if you make a qualifying purchase. That cookie is set and controlled by Amazon, not by us, and Amazon's use of information is covered by the Amazon Privacy Notice. We don't receive your name, address, or payment details from Amazon.

Your choices: You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie notice [where available], and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

We do not sell your personal information.

Email list

Our email list is managed by a third-party email service provider [MailerLite / Kit / Mailchimp]. Your email address is stored with that provider so we can send you our emails. We only share information with service providers that help us run the Site, such as our web host, analytics provider, and email service provider, and only as needed for them to provide their services. We keep your email address for as long as you're subscribed. We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. Analytics data is kept according to our analytics provider's retention settings.

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.
