<h2 id="how-to-shop-for-platform-clogs">How to shop for platform clogs</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Height vs. stability:</strong> A lower platform (around an inch or so) is easier to walk in all day. Taller platforms look bolder but take a little getting used to.</li>
<li><strong>Check the strap:</strong> A heel strap (like the Crocs) or an adjustable buckle helps the clog stay put, which matters more when you&#39;re up higher.</li>
<li><strong>Weight:</strong> Foam platforms (Crocs, EVA soles) feel light. Denser soles feel sturdier but heavier.</li>
<li><strong>Sizing:</strong> Platforms can fit differently than a brand&#39;s flat version. Read the newest reviews for sizing notes before you pick a size.</li>
<li><strong>Watch your step:</strong> Take it easy on stairs and wet surfaces until you&#39;re used to the extra height.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-to-style-platform-clogs">How to style platform clogs</h2>
<p>Platforms look great with wide-leg or straight jeans that just skim the top of the clog, and with midi skirts and tights. Chunky ribbed socks in cream or rust bring out the fall feel. For more outfit formulas, see <a href="/how-to-style-clogs-fall/">How to Style Clogs This Fall</a>.</p>
