1. Crocs Women's Classic Platform Clog: Easiest everyday platform

Why it's here: Crocs describes this as a version of its Classic clog with "a heightened, contoured outsole" and "a slimmer, sleeker look." The backstrap holds Jibbitz charms, so you can personalize it, which fits right in with 2026's customization trend (Byrdie). The Amazon listing lists the platform height at 1.6 inches.

Best for: errands, weekends, and anyone who wants height without giving up that light Crocs feel.

Good to know: Buyer reviews on fit are mixed, and a number of reviewers say the Platform runs smaller than the regular Classic. If you're between sizes, going up is the safer bet. Color and size availability on Amazon changes often.

How to shop for platform clogs

  • Height vs. stability: A lower platform (around an inch or so) is easier to walk in all day. Taller platforms look bolder but take a little getting used to.
  • Check the strap: A heel strap (like the Crocs) or an adjustable buckle helps the clog stay put, which matters more when you're up higher.
  • Weight: Foam platforms (Crocs, EVA soles) feel light. Denser soles feel sturdier but heavier.
  • Sizing: Platforms can fit differently than a brand's flat version. Read the newest reviews for sizing notes before you pick a size.
  • Watch your step: Take it easy on stairs and wet surfaces until you're used to the extra height.

How to style platform clogs

Platforms look great with wide-leg or straight jeans that just skim the top of the clog, and with midi skirts and tights. Chunky ribbed socks in cream or rust bring out the fall feel. For more outfit formulas, see How to Style Clogs This Fall.
