<h2 id="lined-clog-buying-tips">Lined clog buying tips</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Size up if the brand suggests it.</strong> Linings take up room.</li>
<li><strong>Real shearling vs. faux fur:</strong> Real shearling is warmer and more breathable, and it usually costs more. Faux fur is easier to care for.</li>
