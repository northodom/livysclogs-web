Lined clog buying tips

  1. Size up if the brand suggests it. Linings take up room.
  2. Real shearling vs. faux fur: Real shearling is warmer and more breathable, and it usually costs more. Faux fur is easier to care for.
  3. Indoor or outdoor? Look for a sturdy sole (EVA or rubber) if you'll wear them outside.
  4. Suede care: Use a suede brush and protector spray before the first wear.
