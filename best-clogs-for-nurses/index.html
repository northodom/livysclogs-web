1. Dansko Women's Professional Clog: Best overall

Dansko calls the Professional its "flagship" clog. It has:

  • a roomy reinforced toe box
  • a contoured footbed
  • a padded instep collar
  • a protective heel counter
  • an anti-fatigue rocker bottom that helps you move forward as you walk

It carries the APMA Seal of Acceptance (Dansko).

  • Fit tip: Dansko says the Professional is supposed to feel loose. Your heel should move up and down freely, with about a pinky finger's width of space behind it. It's sold in European sizes, so check the brand's chart (Dansko fit info).

What to look for in a work clog

  • Closed toe and heel if your job involves spills or sharp objects.
  • Slip resistance: look for stated standards (for example ASTM F2913) if your workplace requires it.
  • Rocker or contoured sole: helps with long hours standing and walking.
  • Easy to clean: leather can be wiped; foam can be rinsed.
  • Fit: clogs fit differently from sneakers, so read each brand's guide.
