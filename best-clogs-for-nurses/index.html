Cooks, servers, dishwashers, bakers, and managers spend their shifts standing and walking, sometimes across floors that can get wet or greasy. A work clog for that job has to protect the foot, stay put, clean up easily, and meet whatever footwear rule the employer sets. These are three closed-back clogs to consider, described from their makers' own information.

Birkenstock Profi Birki 2.0

Birkenstock describes the Profi Birki 2.0 as "designed with kitchen environments in mind." Its product page lists a lugged, certified slip-resistant outsole with self-cleaning, bone-shaped lugs; a waterproof, fluid-resistant polyurethane upper that is easy to care for; a replaceable, contoured footbed with a microfiber lining; regular and wide widths; and the rating EN ISO 20347:2022 O4 SR FO (Birkenstock).

Keep in mind: that rating is a European standard. The page does not name a US ASTM standard, so check whether your employer asks for one.

Dansko Women's Professional

Dansko calls the Professional its "flagship style." Its product page lists a roomy reinforced toe box, a contoured footbed with built-in natural arch support, a padded instep collar, a heel counter that lets the heel move while giving lateral stability, an inner frame, and an anti-fatigue PU rocker bottom. Dansko lists it as APMA Accepted, with a 2-inch heel. The black patent version linked here has what Dansko calls "patent leather that's easy to wipe clean" (Dansko).

  • Fit: Dansko says "they're supposed to be loose." Leave a pinky's width of space between your heel and the back lining (Dansko FAQ), let the heel move up and down freely, and make sure your toes never press the front. Dansko sizes in EU whole sizes only, so each pair of US half sizes shares one EU size; use the Dansko size chart.
  • Keep in mind: Dansko's product page for the Professional does not make a slip-resistance claim. If your workplace requires slip-resistant footwear, choose on that basis.

Crocs On The Clock

Crocs says the On The Clock has an "enclosed toe and heel" that meets workplace standards, with enhanced arch support, Dual Crocs Comfort cushioning, and a relaxed fit that tapers at the back of the heel. Its grip specs are Crocs Lock slip-resistant treads, ASTM F3445-24 certification, CE certification, and EN ISO 20347:2012 OB SRC, and Crocs names the ASTM F2913-24 test method (Crocs). For sizing, Crocs' general size chart says to round up if you're between sizes (Crocs size chart).

  • Keep in mind: Crocs' product page gives no cleaning instructions for this model.

What to look for in a kitchen or restaurant work clog

  • Closed toe and closed heel. A closed heel helps keep the clog in place as you move, and a closed toe covers the foot against spills.
  • Your employer's slip rule. If it names a standard, such as ASTM F2913, match that exact standard on the maker's page. Makers name different standards, and a line-wide claim does not always cover every model.
  • An upper you can wipe down. Check the maker's care instructions for the exact version you buy.
  • Fit from the maker's own guide. Clogs fit differently from sneakers, and each brand explains its fit differently.
