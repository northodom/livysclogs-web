<p>The original Crocs clog. The Amazon listing describes it as incredibly light, water-friendly, and buoyant, with Iconic Crocs Comfort cushioning, and says you can customize it with Jibbitz charms. It&#39;s great for the drive home or around the house.</p>

<h2 id="what-to-look-for-in-a-work-clog">What to look for in a work clog</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Closed toe and heel</strong> if your job involves spills or sharp objects.</li>
<li><strong>Slip resistance:</strong> look for stated standards (for example ASTM F2913) if your workplace requires it.</li>
<li><strong>Rocker or contoured sole:</strong> helps with long hours standing and walking.</li>
<li><strong>Easy to clean:</strong> leather can be wiped; foam can be rinsed.</li>
<li><strong>Fit:</strong> clogs fit differently from sneakers, so read each brand&#39;s guide.</li>
</ul>
