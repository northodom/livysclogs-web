<h2 id="how-to-choose-a-fall-clog">How to choose a fall clog</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Material:</strong> Suede feels the most &quot;fall.&quot; Oiled leather handles wet weather better. Foam clogs (Crocs) are the easiest to clean.</li>
<li><strong>Lining:</strong> Shearling or faux fur adds warmth but takes up room inside, so size up if the brand suggests it.</li>
<li><strong>Sole:</strong> Flat is classic. Platforms give you height. Rocker soles (Dansko) help if you stand all day.</li>
<li><strong>Color:</strong> Cognac, chestnut, chocolate, and burgundy are this season&#39;s shades (<a href="https://www.dsw.com/articles/inspiration/fall-shoe-trends" rel="noopener" target="_blank">DSW</a>).</li>
