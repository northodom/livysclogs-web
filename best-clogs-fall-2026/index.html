How to choose a fall clog

  • Material: Suede feels the most "fall." Oiled leather handles wet weather better. Foam clogs (Crocs) are the easiest to clean.
  • Lining: Shearling or faux fur adds warmth but takes up room inside, so size up if the brand suggests it.
  • Sole: Flat is classic. Platforms give you height. Rocker soles (Dansko) help if you stand all day.
  • Color: Cognac, chestnut, chocolate, and burgundy are this season's shades (DSW).
