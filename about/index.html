About Livy's Clogs

Livy's Clogs is a specialist guide to clogs and closely related work shoes for restaurant and food-service crews: chefs, line and prep cooks, dishwashers, bakers and pastry cooks, and servers. We are not a general shoe store, and we don't sell anything ourselves.

What we do

  • One shoe: we focus on a single premium kitchen clog, the Dansko Women's Professional in Black Patent, instead of ranking many shoes.
  • One source for product facts: what we say about the clog comes only from Dansko's own product page for it. We quote or closely follow Dansko's wording and don't add claims of our own.
  • Chicago restaurant areas: our guides are written for people who work in Chicago restaurants, starting with River North and the Gold Coast, with Near North coming soon.

We haven't personally tested the shoes we list. Product details come from the brands and their Amazon listings, and we don't list prices, because they change on Amazon.
