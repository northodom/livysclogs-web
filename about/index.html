About Livy's Clogs

Livy's Clogs is a specialist guide to clogs and closely related work shoes for restaurant and food-service crews: chefs, line and prep cooks, dishwashers, bakers and pastry cooks, and servers. We are not a general shoe store, and we don't sell anything ourselves.

What we do

  • Work best sellers: we show Amazon's Health Care & Food Service Best Sellers lists as a dated snapshot, in Amazon's own order, grouped by brand. We don't invent or adjust rankings. See How we rank.
  • Premium brands: a short, hand-picked list of professional clog and work-shoe makers, clearly labelled as our picks rather than a ranking.
  • Guides: fit, care, and brand-lineup guides written from brand information and product listings.

We haven't personally tested the shoes we list. Product details come from the brands and their Amazon listings, and we don't list prices, because they change on Amazon.

How the site is funded
