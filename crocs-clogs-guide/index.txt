13:T103a,<p>Crocs makes many clogs, but only some are built for work. This guide covers two work styles for kitchen and restaurant staff: the On The Clock and the Bistro. They differ most at the back of the foot. The On The Clock has an enclosed heel. The Bistro is open at the back, with a heel strap. Classic Crocs with vent holes are not covered here.</p>

<li><strong>Crocs Bistro (open back with heel strap):</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0016J81B4?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check price on Amazon</a></li>

<h2 id="crocs-on-the-clock">Crocs On The Clock</h2>
<p>Crocs describes the On The Clock with an &quot;enclosed toe and heel&quot; that it says meets workplace standards, along with enhanced arch support and Dual Crocs Comfort cushioning. It says the fit is relaxed, with a tapered fit at the back of the heel to hold the foot securely. For grip, Crocs lists Crocs Lock slip-resistant treads, ASTM F3445-24 certification, CE certification, and EN ISO 20347:2012 OB SRC, and names the ASTM F2913-24 test method (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/p/crocs-on-the-clock-work-slip-on/205073.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs</a>).</p>
<p>Crocs&#39; product page doesn&#39;t give cleaning instructions for this model.</p>
<p><strong>Consider it if:</strong> your workplace requires a shoe with a closed back, or you want a slip-resistant work clog with a relaxed fit.</p>

<h2 id="crocs-bistro">Crocs Bistro</h2>
<p>Crocs describes the Bistro with an enclosed toe design and a thicker metatarsal area that help protect the foot from spills, contoured footbeds, and what it calls Iconic Crocs Comfort: lightweight, flexible, 360-degree comfort. It says the clog is fully molded and easy to clean with soap and water. For grip, Crocs lists Crocs Lock slip-resistant tread, ASTM F3445-24 certification, CE certification, and EN ISO 20347:2012 OB SRC, and names the ASTM F2913-24 test method (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/p/bistro-work-clog/10075.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs</a>).</p>
<p><strong>Open back, heel strap.</strong> The Bistro is not a closed-back clog. Crocs&#39; own product photos show an open back with a strap attached at each side. Some kitchens require shoes with an enclosed heel, so check your employer&#39;s rule before choosing the Bistro. If it requires an enclosed heel, the On The Clock is the Crocs work style that has one.</p>
<p><strong>Consider it if:</strong> your workplace allows an open back with a heel strap, and you want a molded clog you can wash with soap and water.</p>

<h2 id="sizing">Sizing</h2>
<p>Crocs&#39; adult size chart says that if you&#39;re between sizes, round up, and that if you have wide feet, order your normal size (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/size-chart" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs size chart</a>). This advice comes from Crocs&#39; general chart, not from either product page.</p>
<h2 id="before-you-wear-them-to-work">Before you wear them to work</h2>
<p>Check your employer&#39;s footwear rule. If it names a slip-resistance standard, match that exact standard on the product page of the model you buy. If it requires an enclosed heel, choose the On The Clock.</p>
