13:T21c1,<p>Crocs makes a lot more than one clog. There are platforms, cozy lined pairs, recovery clogs, closed work clogs, and kids&#39; sizes, and each one fits a little differently. Here&#39;s what each main style is for, so you can skip the guesswork.</p>
<p><em>How we wrote this: we haven&#39;t wear-tested every style. This guide is based on Crocs&#39; own product and fit information and the current Amazon listings, with sources linked. Fit notes are quoted from each listing because they differ by style.</em></p>
<h2 id="quick-picks">Quick picks</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Best all-around:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001526RFY?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Classic Clog</a></li>
<li><strong>Best for kids:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09643VWH5?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Kids&#39; Classic Clog</a></li>
<li><strong>Best cozy pick:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09P1HTTSJ?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Classic Lined Clog</a></li>
<li><strong>Best for height:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B088ZWHRKH?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs Classic Platform Clog</a></li>
<li><strong>Best for work:</strong> <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B071FBKVBG?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocs On The Clock Clog</a></li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="first-how-crocs-sizing-works">First, how Crocs sizing works</h2>
<p>Crocs describes three fits in its fit guide (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/crocs-fit-guide/fit-guide-landing.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs</a>):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Roomy:</strong> the widest and longest fit, like the original clog. Your feet often won&#39;t touch the sides.</li>
<li><strong>Relaxed:</strong> a little room, but more secure than roomy.</li>
<li><strong>Standard:</strong> snug but not tight, with little or no slipping.</li>
</ul>
<p>The Amazon listings give style-by-style advice, and it doesn&#39;t all point the same way. Some styles say size down and others say size up, so read the fit note for the exact style you want.</p>
<h2 id="the-everyday-clogs">The everyday clogs</h2>
<h3 id="crocs-classic-clog">Crocs Classic Clog</h3>
<p>The one that started it all. Crocs says it&#39;s light, water-friendly, easy to clean, and has pivoting heel straps. Its product page says a pair &quot;holds 26 Jibbitz&quot; (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/p/classic-clog/10001.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs</a>).</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> errands, the beach, the garden, and just about everything else.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fit note (listing):</strong> &quot;Runs large. Size down if between sizes.&quot;</li>
</ul>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001526RFY?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Classic Clog on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h3 id="crocs-kids-classic-clog">Crocs Kids&#39; Classic Clog</h3>
<p>The same shoe in toddler, little kid, and big kid sizes, with vent holes and pivoting heel straps. It&#39;s easy for kids to put on themselves, and they can decorate it with Jibbitz charms.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> school-age kids, the backyard, and splash pads.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09643VWH5?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Kids&#39; Classic Clog on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h3 id="crocband-and-bayaband">Crocband and Bayaband</h3>
<p>These are the sporty versions with a stripe around the sole. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CT27XG47?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crocband</a> listing recommends sizing up. The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07FKXSPM9?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bayaband</a> adds perforated detail and takes Jibbitz.</p>
<h3 id="baya">Baya</h3>
<p>The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CJDPK58X?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Baya</a> is a twist on the Classic with extra ventilation. Its listing says it helps drain water and debris, and it recommends sizing down.</p>
<h2 id="the-cozy-clog">The cozy clog</h2>
<h3 id="crocs-classic-lined-clog">Crocs Classic Lined Clog</h3>
<p>This is the Classic with a fuzzy liner, and it works as a slipper that can handle quick errands. The listing says it&#39;s roomy and suggests ordering up to the next whole size. We cover it in depth in our Classic Lined review.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> chilly mornings and house-shoe duty.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09P1HTTSJ?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Classic Lined on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h2 id="the-platforms">The platforms</h2>
<h3 id="crocs-classic-platform-clog">Crocs Classic Platform Clog</h3>
<p>This is the Classic upper on a 1.6-inch (41mm) wedge, according to the listing, and it holds charms on the backstrap too.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fit note (listing):</strong> &quot;Runs small. Size up if between sizes.&quot;</li>
</ul>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B088ZWHRKH?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the Classic Platform on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<p><strong>Want more height?</strong> The <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09GH1NBW7?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Crush Clog</a> is listed with a 2-inch height, and that listing recommends sizing up. See our platform clog guide for more options.</p>
<h2 id="the-comfort-first-styles">The comfort-first styles</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BSG41GCC?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Echo Clog</a>:</strong> a sporty clog with a 1.5-inch height boost, a LiteRide drop-in footbed, and a padded heel strap. The listing says it runs large.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BSKH287D?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Mellow Recovery Clog</a>:</strong> deep, cupped footbeds with &quot;sink-in&quot; softness. The listing calls the fit roomy and recommends sizing down.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BX4XGB61?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Dylan Clog</a>:</strong> a Crocs take on the leather mule, with deep heel cups. The listing says it runs large.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-work-clogs">The work clogs</h2>
<h3 id="crocs-on-the-clock-clog">Crocs On The Clock Clog</h3>
<p>A closed toe and heel plus Crocs Lock slip-resistant tread, built for kitchens and hospitals. The listing says it runs large. The closed-toe <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0016J81B4?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Bistro</a> is another option, with extra coverage over the top of the foot. Always check your employer&#39;s footwear rules. See our <a href="/best-clogs-for-nurses/">nurses&#39; clog guide</a> for more.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> shifts where vent holes aren&#39;t allowed.</p>
<p class="cta"><a class="amz" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B071FBKVBG?tag=YOURTAG-20" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank">Check the On The Clock on Amazon</a><span class="cta-note">Opens Amazon.com · price and availability shown there</span></p>
<h2 id="care-in-one-line">Care in one line</h2>
<p>Rinse or wipe with mild soap, air dry, and keep Crocs away from high heat like hot cars, which Crocs says can shrink or warp them (<a href="https://www.crocs.com/customer-service/faqs.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Crocs FAQ</a>).</p>
<h2 id="which-crocs-should-you-get">Which Crocs should you get?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>One pair for everything:</strong> Classic Clog.</li>
<li><strong>Cold feet:</strong> Classic Lined.</li>
<li><strong>A little lift:</strong> Classic Platform, or the Crush for more.</li>
<li><strong>Tired feet after a long day:</strong> Mellow Recovery.</li>
<li><strong>Work shifts:</strong> On The Clock.</li>
</ul>
