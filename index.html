The shoe: Dansko Professional in Black Patent, women's sizing

What Dansko's Black Patent Professional page lists:

  • A patent leather upper that Dansko describes as "easy to wipe clean."
  • A "roomy, reinforced toe box," a padded instep collar and a leather socklining.
  • A closed back with a heel counter, and a contoured footbed.
  • A 2 inch heel height and a 0.8 inch platform.
  • Sizing in European (EU) whole sizes, under Dansko's Women's Clogs. Regular is the only width shown for this color.

Check your restaurant's footwear rule before you buy, and confirm your size and color on the Amazon listing. We haven't worn these clogs ourselves; these details come from Dansko's Professional Black Patent page. Livy's Clogs is not affiliated with Dansko.
