Dansko calls the Professional its "flagship style" and describes its "iconic silhouette." It is a closed-back clog on a rocker bottom, and it is meant to fit differently from a sneaker. This guide sets out what Dansko says is inside it, how Dansko says it should fit, and what to check before wearing it to work.

  • Shop it: Dansko Women's Professional Clog, black patent (women's sizing; check your size and color on the listing) · Check price on Amazon

What's inside the Professional

Dansko's product page for the Professional lists (Dansko):

  • A roomy, reinforced toe box
  • A contoured footbed with built-in natural arch support
  • A padded instep collar
  • A protective heel counter that lets the heel move while giving lateral stability
  • An inner frame for added stability
  • An anti-fatigue PU rocker bottom that Dansko says "promotes forward motion for shock absorption and energy return," with a wide heel strike that "assists with stability"

The same page lists the Professional as APMA Accepted and gives a heel height of 2 inches and a platform of 0.8 inches.

In its clog guide, which compares the Professional with the XP 2.0, Dansko says the Professional is built with "a firmer footbed and a more structured feel" (Dansko clog guide).

Care

The listing linked here is the black patent version. Dansko describes that upper as "patent leather that's easy to wipe clean," and says to wipe it gently with a damp cloth, using soap and water for tougher blemishes (Dansko). Other colorways use other uppers, so check the care note for any other version you buy.

How the Dansko Professional should fit

Dansko's own guidance is short: "they're supposed to be loose." For closed-back clogs, Dansko says (Dansko size guide; Dansko):

  1. The fit may feel slightly snug when you first step in.
  2. Your toes should not touch the front of the clog.
  3. Your heel should move up and down freely as you walk.
  4. Leave about a pinky finger's width of space between your heel and the back lining (Dansko FAQ).

Sizing basics

  • European whole sizes. Dansko says its shoes are sized "according to European (EU) whole size standards" (Dansko size guide), so each pair of US half sizes maps to one EU size. On its chart, a women's US 7½ and 8 are both a 38, and a women's US 8½ and 9 are both a 39. Use the Dansko size chart rather than your sneaker size.
  • Widths. Dansko says most styles are made in medium width, with narrow and wide offered in noted styles. The black patent Professional page offers regular width only (Dansko).
  • Between sizes. Dansko's FAQ says it offers free shipping and free exchanges on full-priced footwear bought from Dansko if you're between sizes (Dansko FAQ).

Before you wear it to work

  • Slip resistance. Dansko makes no slip-resistance claim for the Professional on its product page. If your workplace requires slip-resistant or certified footwear, check the listing against that rule before you buy; the Professional isn't the pick on that basis alone. Footwear rules vary by workplace, and our River North kitchen guide picks the black patent Professional for chefs and line cooks. Our Dansko clogs guide covers the rest of the work lineup.
  • Height and sole shape. With a 2-inch heel and a rocker bottom, it is not a flat shoe. The feel underfoot is different from a sneaker, so try it on at home before a full shift.

Livy's Clogs isn't a medical provider. If you have foot, knee, or back pain, talk with a podiatrist about the right shoe for you.

The short version

Dansko describes the Professional as a structured, closed-back clog with a reinforced toe box, heel counter, and rocker bottom. The fit that matters most, according to Dansko, is roomy at the heel and never tight at the toes, sized from the EU chart.
