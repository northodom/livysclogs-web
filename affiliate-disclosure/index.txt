<h2 id="what-that-means-in-plain-english">What that means, in plain English</h2>
<p>Livy&#39;s Clogs is a free, reader-supported website. Many of the links on this site go to product pages on Amazon.com. These are &quot;affiliate links.&quot; If you click one and then buy something on Amazon, Amazon may pay us a small commission.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>It costs you nothing extra.</strong> You pay the same price on Amazon whether you use our link or not.</li>
<li><strong>We don&#39;t sell anything ourselves.</strong> We don&#39;t hold inventory, ship products, or handle payments. Every purchase happens on Amazon, under Amazon&#39;s terms, returns policy, and customer service.</li>
<li><strong>Commissions help keep the lights on.</strong> They pay for hosting, research time, and keeping our guides up to date.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-we-choose-what-to-recommend">How we choose what to recommend</h2>
<p>Earning a commission doesn&#39;t change what we say about a product. Our goal is to be honest and useful, even when that means pointing out downsides or suggesting you skip something.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Research-based picks.</strong> Unless we clearly say otherwise, our picks are based on research of product listings, brand information, published reviews coverage, and trend reporting. We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless we say so on that page.</li>
<li><strong>No paid placements.</strong> Brands don&#39;t pay us to be included in our guides. If that ever changes for a specific post, we&#39;ll label it clearly on that post.</li>
<li><strong>We link our sources.</strong> When we share a fact about a product (materials, fit guidance, care instructions), we try to link to where it came from, like the brand&#39;s website.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="about-prices-and-availability">About prices and availability</h2>
<p>We don&#39;t list prices on this site. Prices, colors, sizes, and availability on Amazon change often, so please check Amazon for the current details before you buy.</p>
