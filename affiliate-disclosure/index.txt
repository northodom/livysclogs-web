<h2 id="what-that-means-in-plain-english">What that means, in plain English</h2>
<p>Livy&#39;s Clogs is a free, reader-supported website. Many of the links on this site go to product pages on Amazon.com. These are &quot;affiliate links.&quot; If you click one and then buy something on Amazon, Amazon may pay us a small commission.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>It costs you nothing extra.</strong> You pay the same price on Amazon whether you use our link or not.</li>
<li><strong>We don&#39;t sell anything ourselves.</strong> We don&#39;t hold inventory, ship products, or handle payments. Every purchase happens on Amazon, under Amazon&#39;s terms, returns policy, and customer service.</li>
<li><strong>Commissions help keep the site running.</strong> They pay for hosting, research time, and keeping our guides up to date.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-we-choose-what-to-show">How we choose what to show</h2>
<p>Earning a commission doesn&#39;t change what we say about a product.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rankings come from Amazon.</strong> Our best-seller lists are a dated snapshot of Amazon&#39;s public Best Sellers lists, shown in Amazon&#39;s order. We don&#39;t invent or adjust rankings. See <a href="/how-we-rank/">How we rank</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Premium brands are our picks.</strong> That list is hand-picked, labelled as editorial, and not a ranking.</li>
<li><strong>Research-based, not tested.</strong> Our guides are based on product listings and brand information. We do not claim to have personally tested a product.</li>
<li><strong>No paid placements.</strong> Brands don&#39;t pay us to be included. If that ever changes for a specific page, we&#39;ll label it clearly on that page.</li>
</ul>
