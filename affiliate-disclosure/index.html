What that means, in plain English

Livy's Clogs is a free, reader-supported website. Many of the links on this site go to product pages on Amazon.com. These are "affiliate links." If you click one and then buy something on Amazon, Amazon may pay us a small commission.

  • It costs you nothing extra. You pay the same price on Amazon whether you use our link or not.
  • We don't sell anything ourselves. We don't hold inventory, ship products, or handle payments. Every purchase happens on Amazon, under Amazon's terms, returns policy, and customer service.
  • Commissions help keep the site running. They pay for hosting, research time, and keeping our guides up to date.

Other affiliate programs

Besides Amazon, some links on this site may go to other retailers or brands whose affiliate programs we participate in. Those links work the same way: if you buy through one, we may earn a commission, and you pay the same price. The same editorial rules below apply to every link, whatever the retailer.

How we choose what to show

Earning a commission doesn't change what we say about a product.

  • Rankings come from Amazon. Our best-seller lists are a dated snapshot of Amazon's public Best Sellers lists, shown in Amazon's order. We don't invent or adjust rankings. See How we rank.
  • Premium brands are our picks. That list is hand-picked, labelled as editorial, and not a ranking.
  • Research-based, not tested. Our guides are based on product listings and brand information. We do not claim to have personally tested a product.
  • No paid placements. Brands don't pay us to be included. If that ever changes for a specific page, we'll label it clearly on that page.

About prices and availability

We don't list prices on this site. Prices, colors, sizes, and availability on Amazon change often, so please check Amazon for the current details before you buy.

Where you'll see this disclosure

Besides this page, the disclosure appears at the top of every page of the site and above the first affiliate link in every article.
