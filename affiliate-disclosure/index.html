What that means, in plain English

Livy's Clogs is a free, reader-supported website. Many of the links on this site go to product pages on Amazon.com. These are "affiliate links." If you click one and then buy something on Amazon, Amazon may pay us a small commission.

  • It costs you nothing extra. You pay the same price on Amazon whether you use our link or not.
  • We don't sell anything ourselves. We don't hold inventory, ship products, or handle payments. Every purchase happens on Amazon, under Amazon's terms, returns policy, and customer service.
  • Commissions help keep the lights on. They pay for hosting, research time, and keeping our guides up to date.

How we choose what to recommend

Earning a commission doesn't change what we say about a product. Our goal is to be honest and useful, even when that means pointing out downsides or suggesting you skip something.

  • Research-based picks. Unless we clearly say otherwise, our picks are based on research of product listings, brand information, published reviews coverage, and trend reporting. We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless we say so on that page.
  • No paid placements. Brands don't pay us to be included in our guides. If that ever changes for a specific post, we'll label it clearly on that post.
  • We link our sources. When we share a fact about a product (materials, fit guidance, care instructions), we try to link to where it came from, like the brand's website.

About prices and availability

We don't list prices on this site. Prices, colors, sizes, and availability on Amazon change often, so please check Amazon for the current details before you buy.

Where you'll see this disclosure
