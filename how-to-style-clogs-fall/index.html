Clogs are the fall 2026 shoe that goes with almost everything. They sit right between summer sandals and winter boots, and you can wear them bare-ankled on mild days and add socks as it gets colder (Claire Mac). DSW says they look especially good with straight-leg or wide-leg denim (DSW). Here are eight outfit formulas to try.

1. Wide-leg jeans + cozy knit + suede clogs

The easiest fall outfit there is. Let the hem sit just over the top of the clog. Try it with Birkenstock Boston Soft Footbed or the CUSHIONAIRE Hobby.

2. Straight jeans + chunky socks

Cream or rust ribbed socks with brown clogs is the fall look. For extra warmth, go with a lined pair like the Birkenstock Boston Shearling.

3. Midi or maxi dress + clogs

Stylists pair chunky clogs with flowy dresses for contrast. Sandra Bullock recently wore studded clogs with a maxi gown and a blazer (Us Weekly).

4. All one color

Byrdie notes that a mostly one-color outfit makes chunky clogs look sleek and high-fashion (Byrdie). Try chocolate on chocolate, or all cream with tan clogs.

5. Tailored trousers + clogs for the office

A relaxed trouser and a crisp shirt with a leather clog keeps it polished. For long workdays, see our work clog guide.

6. Leggings + oversized sweater + lined clogs

Weekend errands, sorted. The Crocs Classic Lined Clog is the cozy, easy option.

7. Platform clogs + mini skirt + tights

Platforms add height and a little '70s attitude. Try the Crocs Classic Platform.

8. Add charms or pops of color

Customization is a big part of 2026's clog trend (Byrdie). Add a Crocs Jibbitz 5-pack of charms to your Crocs, or pick an animal-print or burgundy accent (both are fall 2026 trends per DSW).

Fall 2026 color cheat sheet

  • Cognac, caramel, chestnut, chocolate: go with cream, denim, olive, and navy.
  • Burgundy: goes with gray, black, camel, and denim.
